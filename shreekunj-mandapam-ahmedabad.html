HOSPITALITY RESORT | LANDSCAPE DESIGN

Shreekunj Mandapam Ahmedabad

"A premium event and banquet complex where our landscape design creates memorable settings for cultural celebrations. We integrated traditional garden elements with contemporary event infrastructure, offering versatile outdoor venues that honor Gujarat’s rich celebratory traditions while enhancing the overall hospitality experience."

The Vision

Shreekunj Mandapam, Ahmedabad was envisioned as a premium hospitality and cultural event destination where landscape design enhances the grandeur of celebrations through elegant outdoor settings and functional gathering spaces. Our approach focused on blending traditional garden aesthetics with contemporary event infrastructure to create memorable environments that reflect Gujarat’s rich cultural and celebratory traditions.

Key Highlights

Event venue landscape architecture
Cultural event outdoor space design
Integration of traditional and contemporary garden elements
Landscape supporting diverse celebration formats
