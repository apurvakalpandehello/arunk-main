RESIDENTIAL PROJECT & LANDSCAPE DESIGN

Mahindra Woods Pune

"A thoughtfully planned residential township where our landscape design establishes vibrant community environments while reflecting Mahindra Lifespaces’ quality standards. We integrated recreational amenities, ecological green corridors, and open social spaces to create a lifestyle that balances urban convenience with a strong connection to nature."

The Vision

Mahindra Woods, Pune was envisioned as a thoughtfully planned residential township where landscape design enhances community living through integrated green spaces, recreational environments, and sustainable outdoor experiences. The project aimed to establish Mahindra Lifespaces’ quality standards while creating a harmonious balance between urban lifestyle, ecology, and resident well-being.

Key Highlights

Residential township landscape architecture
Mahindra Lifespaces brand landscape standards
Community recreation and green infrastructure
Sustainable residential landscape practices

Key Challenges

↗
