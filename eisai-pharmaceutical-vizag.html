INDUSTRIAL & CORPORATE | LANDSCAPE DESIGN

EISAI Pharmaceutical Visakhapatnam

"A pharmaceutical manufacturing facility where our landscape design enhances corporate identity while supporting industrial functionality. We developed sustainable green infrastructure that reflects EISAI’s commitment to innovation, quality, and environmental responsibility."

The Vision

EISAI Pharmaceutical, Visakhapatnam was envisioned as a modern pharmaceutical manufacturing campus where landscape design strengthens corporate identity, operational efficiency, and environmental responsibility. Our approach focused on creating sustainable green infrastructure and professional outdoor environments that support industrial functionality while enhancing employee wellbeing and campus aesthetics.

Key Highlights

Pharmaceutical campus landscape design
Sustainable green infrastructure for industrial setting
Corporate image enhancement through landscape
Coastal climate-responsive landscape solutions
