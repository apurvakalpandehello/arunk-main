MIX USE TOWNSHIP | LANDSCAPE DESIGN

Lavasa Township Phase-I Pune

"A pioneering hill city township where our landscape design creates vibrant public spaces, green infrastructure, and connected outdoor environments that enhance urban living and township identity."

The Vision

Lavasa Township Phase-I, Pune was envisioned as India’s pioneering planned hill city where landscape design plays a defining role in shaping urban identity, public life, and environmental sustainability. Our approach focused on creating vibrant public realms, integrated green infrastructure, and outdoor environments that enhance the township’s mixed-use character while responding to the natural hill station setting.

Key Highlights

Landscape design for India's first planned hill city
Mixed-use township public realm and green infrastructure
Hill station urban landscape vocabulary
Integration with Lavasa's master-planned vision

Key Challenges

↗

Designing public landscapes that support mixed-use township life while maintaining a cohesive urban identity
