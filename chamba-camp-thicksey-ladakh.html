HOSPITALITY RESORT | ARCHITECTURAL, INTERIOR & LANDSCAPE DESIGN

Chamba Camp, Thicksey

"A luxury wilderness retreat where our contemporary design meets Himalayan grandeur. Our comprehensive approach—spanning architecture, interiors, and landscape—creates an immersive hospitality experience that respects Ladakh's fragile ecology while delivering world-class comfort in one of the world's most dramatic landscapes."

The Vision

Luxury refined by altitude, the Chamba Camp at Thiksey was envisioned as a sanctuary that bridges the gap between raw, untouched wilderness and sophisticated hospitality. The project aims to offer a transcendent guest experience that feels entirely harmonious with the stark, breathtaking beauty of the Ladakhi landscape, emphasizing lightweight, sustainable construction and deep cultural integration.

Key Highlights

Full-spectrum design: architecture, interiors, and landscape
Sustainable hospitality design in high-altitude environment
Integration with Ladakhi terrain and cultural context
Premium guest experience within wilderness setting

Key Challenges

↗

Designing structures resilient to the extreme climate and high-altitude conditions of Ladakh.

↗

Maintaining a zero-footprint approach to preserve the fragile desert ecology.

↗

Blending contemporary luxury standards with authentic, locally-inspired design aesthetics.
