RESIDENTIAL PROJECT | LANDSCAPE DESIGN

Akshar Greenworld, Navi Mumbai

"A residential project where our landscape design fulfills the development's naming promise—creating genuinely green living environments. We established extensive landscaping that defines Akshar Greenworld's market positioning, delivering residents substantial green infrastructure and recreational amenities."

The Vision

Green living, redefined. The landscape architecture for Akshar Greenworld was conceptualized to serve as the project's primary identity. The vision was to transform the residential experience by embedding nature into the everyday lives of residents, ensuring that 'Greenworld' was not merely a name, but a tangible, expansive reality that enhances well-being and property value in the context of Navi Mumbai's urban growth.

Key Highlights

Green-focused residential landscape design
Extensive landscaping as project USP
Resident amenities and outdoor programming
Navi Mumbai residential development standards

Key Challenges

↗

Scaling intensive green landscape elements to cover a significant project area without compromising structural integrity.

↗

Creating a cohesive ecological aesthetic that feels like a 'world' rather than isolated patchworks of greenery.

↗

Balancing high-density residential requirements with the necessity for expansive, usable open spaces.
