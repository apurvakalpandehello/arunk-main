HOSPITALITY RESORT & LANDSCAPE DESIGN

Rhythm Resort Lonavala

"A wellness-oriented resort where our landscape design supports holistic guest experiences. Nestled in Lonavala's monsoon-green hills, we created outdoor environments that facilitate relaxation, recreation, and connection with the Sahyadri's natural rhythms."

The Vision

Rhythm Resort, Lonavala was envisioned as a wellness-driven hospitality destination seamlessly connected with the natural beauty of the Sahyadri hills. The landscape design was carefully planned to create calming outdoor environments that encourage relaxation, recreation, and immersive guest experiences while celebrating the lush monsoon character of Lonavala.

Key Highlights

Landscape design for wellness-focused resort
Integration with Lonavala's natural hill station character
Outdoor spaces supporting health and recreation programs
Monsoon-responsive planting and water features

Key Challenges

↗

Designing outdoor experiences that remain functional and visually engaging throughout Lonavala’s heavy monsoon climate

↗

Preserving the site’s natural topography while integrating resort pathways, recreational zones, and wellness spaces

↗

Creating serene and private outdoor environments that enhance guest comfort and connection with nature

Project Brief

PROJECT TYPE Hospitality Resort
LOCATION Lonavala, Maharashtra
SERVICE Landscape Design

Our Solutions

  • ✓ Designed wellness-oriented outdoor spaces promoting relaxation and guest interaction
  • ✓ Integrated native plantation and water-sensitive landscape elements suitable for Lonavala’s monsoon climate
  • ✓ Created recreational pathways and open gathering areas that connect guests with the surrounding natural environment
