HOSPITALITY RESORT | LANDSCAPE DESIGN

ITC Narmada, Ahmedabad

"ITC's Gujarat flagship where our landscape design echoes the brand's commitment to responsible luxury. We created outdoor environments that blend traditional Indian garden vocabulary with contemporary sustainability, offering guests an experience rooted in culture and environmental stewardship."

The Vision

Responsible luxury in every detail. The landscape architecture for ITC Narmada was envisioned as a living testament to the brand's ethos of sustainable opulence. The project aims to synthesize the rich cultural tapestry of Gujarat’s garden traditions with modern ecological best practices, ensuring that the property is not merely a destination, but a harmonious extension of the region’s heritage and environment.

Key Highlights

Landscape design for ITC's luxury hospitality brand
Sustainable garden practices and native planting
Cultural landscape elements reflecting regional heritage
Integrated outdoor spaces supporting resort programming

Key Challenges

↗

Designing large-scale luxury gardens that adhere to strict water conservation and sustainability protocols.

↗

Integrating complex cultural narratives and heritage-inspired motifs into a contemporary resort landscape.

↗

Balancing high-performance operational requirements of the resort with the need for serene, immersive guest experiences.

Our Solutions

  • ✓ Incorporated geometric water features and shaded walkways reminiscent of traditional Gujarati garden architecture
  • ✓ Curated public-facing zones that serve as flexible event venues, maximizing both beauty and utility
