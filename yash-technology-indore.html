INDUSTRIAL & CORPORATE | LANDSCAPE DESIGN

Yash Technology Indore

"A corporate campus where our landscape design transforms functional infrastructure into an environment that supports innovation, collaboration, and employee wellbeing. We created outdoor spaces that balance corporate identity with practical campus requirements, delivering green infrastructure that strengthens Indore’s growing technology sector presence."

The Vision

Tropical Retreat, Igatpuri was envisioned as a wellness-focused hospitality destination where master planning, architecture, and landscape design work together to create immersive experiences rooted in nature. Set within the Western Ghats, the project was designed to harmonize with the hill station’s natural ecology while offering guests restorative environments that promote relaxation, wellness, and connection with the surrounding landscape.

Key Highlights

Master planning, architecture, and landscape for wellness resort
Design integration with Western Ghats hill station ecology
Built and landscape environments supporting wellness programming
Sustainable resort development in sensitive natural setting

Key Challenges

↗

Designing resort infrastructure that harmonizes with the natural topography and ecological sensitivity of the Western Ghats
