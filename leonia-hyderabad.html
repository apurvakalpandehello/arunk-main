Leonia / Leo Meridian Resort Hyderabad

"A landmark resort complex where our complete design authorship creates a unified hospitality world. Through integrated master planning, architecture, interiors, and landscape design, we developed a self-contained destination that offers Hyderabad guests immersive experiences extending beyond conventional hospitality environments."

The Vision

Leonia / Leo Meridian Resort, Hyderabad was envisioned as a landmark hospitality destination where master planning, architecture, interiors, and landscape design work together to create a complete and immersive resort environment. Our integrated design approach focused on establishing a distinctive hospitality identity, enhancing guest engagement, and delivering a self-contained destination experience tailored to Hyderabad’s growing leisure and lifestyle market.

Key Highlights

End-to-end design: master planning, architecture, interiors, landscape

Integrated design language across all guest touchpoints
