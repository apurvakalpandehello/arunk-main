The Exotica Thane

"A comprehensive resort development where our multidisciplinary approach creates a distinctive hospitality destination. Through integrated master planning, architecture, interiors, and landscape design, we crafted immersive guest experiences that connect functionality, ambiance, and outdoor environments within Thane’s evolving urban context."

The Vision

The Exotica, Thane was envisioned as a premium hospitality destination where master planning, architecture, interiors, and landscape design come together to create a seamless resort experience. Our multidisciplinary approach focused on establishing a strong site identity, enhancing guest comfort, and creating immersive indoor-outdoor environments that respond to Thane’s evolving urban hospitality market.
