PUBLIC PLACE | MASTER PLANNING & LANDSCAPE DESIGN

Upvan Lake, Thane

"A transformative waterfront intervention that elevates Thane's urban landscape through ecological restoration and community-centric design. Our master planning revitalizes the lake precinct, creating a premium public amenity that balances environmental conservation with recreational functionality."

The Vision

Redefining urban waterfronts, the beautification of Upvan Lake was conceptualized to breathe new life into one of Thane's most cherished natural assets. The vision was to transform the area into a vibrant, sustainable public sanctuary where the sanctity of the water body is protected while providing high-quality, accessible recreational spaces for the growing urban population.

Key Highlights

Comprehensive waterfront master planning and beautification
Ecological landscape design enhancing natural water systems
Community-focused public spaces and waterfront promenades
Integration of recreation, conservation, and urban design

Key Challenges

↗

Balancing the heavy recreational usage of the waterfront with the need for rigorous ecological conservation.

↗

Restoring the natural bank profile while creating functional, safe, and aesthetic public access points.

↗

Managing the interface between urban infrastructure and the sensitive aquatic environment.
