MONUMENTS | MASTER PLANNING & LANDSCAPE DESIGN

Akshardham, Delhi

"An iconic monument defining Delhi's architectural landscape, where our vision transformed expansive grounds into a seamless blend of sacred spaces and lush environments. Our master planning orchestrates movement and experience, while landscape design creates contemplative zones that enhance the monument's spiritual resonance."

The Vision

Designing spaces worthy of legacy, the project was envisioned to anchor a profound spiritual identity within the national capital. By integrating grand architectural vision with the serenity of landscape, the site serves as a transcendent journey for millions, balancing historical reverence with modern functional requirements.

Key Highlights

Master planning of Delhi's premier monument destination
Landscape architecture complementing cultural significance
Large-scale green infrastructure around heritage structures
Thoughtful spatial flow design for ceremonial and visitor areas

Key Challenges

↗

Ensuring the structural integrity and aesthetic continuity of expansive grounds while hosting massive daily footfall.

↗

Harmonizing the immense scale of the monument with intimate, contemplative landscape zones.

↗

Implementing large-scale green infrastructure that thrives in a challenging urban climate while maintaining spiritual sanctity.
