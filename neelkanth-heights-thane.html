RESIDENTIAL PROJECT & LANDSCAPE DESIGN

Neelkanth Heights Thane

"A comprehensive residential development where our integrated design approach spans landscape design, clubhouse architecture, and interior spaces. We created cohesive environments that seamlessly connect apartment tower surroundings with recreational amenities, delivering residents a refined lifestyle experience that balances comfort, recreation, and modern urban living."

The Vision

Neelkanth Heights, Thane was envisioned as a premium residential development where landscape design, clubhouse architecture, and interior spaces work together to create a cohesive lifestyle environment. The project aimed to deliver a refined residential experience that combines recreation, wellness, and modern urban living while elevating the overall quality of community interaction and resident comfort.

Key Highlights

Landscape design with clubhouse architecture and interiors
Integrated residential amenities design
Complete lifestyle environment for tower residents
Thane residential market premium positioning
