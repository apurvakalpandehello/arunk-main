HOSPITALITY RESORT | LANDSCAPE DESIGN

Taj Aravali, Udaipur

"An iconic Taj property where our landscape architecture frames views of Udaipur's lakes and palaces. We created layered outdoor experiences—from intimate courtyards to expansive lawns—that complement the Taj's legendary hospitality with gardens worthy of Rajasthan's royal heritage."

The Vision

Gardens befitting royalty, the landscape design for Taj Aravali was conceived to provide a sensory extension of the resort’s luxurious architecture. The goal was to weave together various outdoor environments that allow guests to traverse through private, tranquil courtyards into open, majestic lawns, all while ensuring the natural beauty of the Aravalli hills and Udaipur’s historic vistas remain the central focal point.

Key Highlights

Landscape design for premium Taj Group property
Garden architecture reflecting Rajasthani palace traditions
Curated outdoor spaces enhancing guest experience
Integration with Udaipur's natural and cultural landscape

Key Challenges

↗

Balancing opulent garden aesthetics with the need for water conservation in a regional arid climate.

↗

Framing iconic regional views through carefully positioned landscape elements and vistas.

↗

Designing diverse outdoor zones that offer both privacy for guests and grandiosity for events.
