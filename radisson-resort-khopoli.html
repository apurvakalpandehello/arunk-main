HOSPITALITY RESORT | LANDSCAPE DESIGN

Radisson Resort, Khopoli

"A contemporary resort nestled in Maharashtra's Western Ghats, where our landscape design leverages natural topography to create diverse outdoor environments. We transformed the resort grounds into a seamless extension of the Sahyadri landscape, offering guests both adventure and tranquility."

The Vision

Where hills meet hospitality, the landscape design for the Radisson Resort in Khopoli was crafted to amplify the dramatic beauty of the Western Ghats. The vision focused on a dialogue between built form and natural terrain, utilizing the site's unique slope and elevation to curate varied, immersive outdoor experiences that respond to the region’s rich biodiversity.

Key Highlights

Landscape design for Radisson hospitality standards
Integration with Western Ghats natural terrain
Resort-wide outdoor amenity planning
Sustainable landscape solutions for hill station setting

Key Challenges

↗

Managing extreme slopes and varying elevations while maintaining ADA accessibility standards.

↗

Creating functional recreational zones that do not compromise the fragile ecosystem of the Sahyadris.

↗

Implementing effective drainage and erosion control measures suited for heavy monsoon rainfall.
