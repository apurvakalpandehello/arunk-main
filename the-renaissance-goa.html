HOSPITALITY RESORT | MASTER PLANNING & LANDSCAPE DESIGN

The Renaissance Goa

"A comprehensive resort development where our master planning and landscape design create a complete destination experience. We leveraged Goa’s coastal ecology and cultural character to deliver Renaissance hospitality through thoughtfully planned outdoor environments, immersive guest experiences, and strategic site organization."

The Vision

The Renaissance, Goa was envisioned as a complete hospitality destination where master planning and landscape design work together to create immersive resort experiences rooted in Goa’s coastal ecology and cultural identity. Our approach focused on organizing the site into well-connected hospitality zones, enhancing outdoor experiences, and creating landscapes that celebrate the relaxed yet vibrant character of Goa.

Key Highlights

Master planning and landscape design for resort complex
Coastal site planning maximizing beach and view access
Integrated resort zones and outdoor programming
Goan cultural landscape vocabulary in contemporary expression

Key Challenges

↗
