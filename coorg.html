NATURE RESORT DESIGN

Forest Retreat, Coorg

"A series of treehouse villas that dissolve into the coffee plantation canopy of Coorg."

The Vision

The Forest Retreat project was envisioned as a tranquil eco-luxury destination immersed within the lush coffee plantations of Coorg. The design creates seamless connections between architecture and nature through elevated treehouse villas, panoramic forest views, and sustainable living experiences.

Key Challenges

↗

Constructing elevated villas without disturbing the natural terrain

↗

Preserving existing coffee plantations and forest biodiversity

↗

Designing weather-resistant structures for heavy rainfall regions

Project Brief

LOCATION Coorg, Karnataka
PROJECT TYPE Eco-Luxury Retreat
YEAR 2023

Our Solutions

  • ✓ Designed lightweight elevated structures with minimal land intervention
  • ✓ Used natural timber, stone, and locally sourced materials
  • ✓ Integrated rainwater harvesting and passive cooling systems
