Our Story

A Legacy of 35+ Years

Arunkumar Designers & Consultants Pvt. Ltd. (ADCPL) was established with a vision to create sustainable and experiential spaces.

Over three decades, we have successfully delivered projects across diverse sectors including tourism, religious developments, and urban environments.

Our Philosophy

Harmony with Nature

Our philosophy is built around the belief that architecture and landscape evolve as a unified expression of the five natural elements — earth, water, fire, air, and space .

We design environments that inspire people while respecting the inherent natural systems of the site.

Core Team

The Minds Behind ADCPL

Led by Mr. Arunkumar, our team brings together experts in architecture, horticulture, environmental conservation, and project management. We believe in a collaborative approach that merges technical precision with creative vision.
