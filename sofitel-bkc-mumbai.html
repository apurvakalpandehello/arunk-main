HOSPITALITY RESORT | LANDSCAPE DESIGN

Sofitel, BKC, Mumbai

"A luxury urban resort in Mumbai's premier business district, where our landscape design creates a verdant sanctuary amidst BKC's corporate intensity. We transformed limited outdoor spaces into high-impact gardens that embody Sofitel's French elegance and Mumbai's cosmopolitan energy."

The Vision

Urban sanctuary, elevated. The landscape architecture for the Sofitel in BKC was driven by the need to carve out a lush, serene refuge within one of India's most intense, high-density corporate environments. The vision was to marry French-inspired sophistication with the vibrant, cosmopolitan pulse of Mumbai, creating an atmosphere of exclusivity and calm that serves as a quiet retreat for global business travelers.

Key Highlights

Premium landscape design for Sofitel luxury standards
Urban garden solutions in high-density business district
Maximized green impact in constrained spaces
Sophisticated outdoor aesthetics for international guests

Key Challenges

↗

Designing high-impact landscape features within a severely restricted urban footprint.

↗

Managing the harsh, reflective urban micro-climate of the business district to ensure plant vitality.

↗

Curating a luxurious, refined aesthetic that maintains its elegance under heavy professional usage.
