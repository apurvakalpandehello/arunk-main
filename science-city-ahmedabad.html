PUBLIC PLACE | MASTER PLANNING & LANDSCAPE DESIGN

Science City, Ahmedabad

"Gujarat's premier science and innovation hub, reimagined through our comprehensive master planning that balances educational functionality with environmental sensitivity. Our landscape design transforms the complex into an engaging public realm where discovery meets green infrastructure, creating spaces that inspire curiosity and community interaction."

The Vision

Innovation rooted in design excellence, the Science City project was envisioned as a landmark destination to foster scientific temper and modern learning. The objective was to craft a multifaceted public realm that harmonizes the intellectual rigor of scientific exploration with a welcoming, verdant environment, ensuring a seamless experience for visitors of all ages.

Key Highlights

Integrated master plan for science and education complex
Landscape design supporting interactive public engagement
Sustainable outdoor learning environments
Strategic zoning for exhibition, recreation, and circulation

Key Challenges

↗

Synthesizing vast educational facilities into a cohesive, navigable master plan.

↗

Developing climate-responsive green infrastructure capable of sustaining high-traffic public zones.

↗

Ensuring landscape design enhances, rather than distracts from, the complex's core scientific mission.
