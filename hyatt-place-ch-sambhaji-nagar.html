HOSPITALITY RESORT | LANDSCAPE DESIGN

Hyatt Place Chhatrapati Sambhaji Nagar

"A contemporary business and leisure hotel where our landscape design creates a welcoming arrival sequence and functional outdoor spaces that align with Hyatt Place’s modern hospitality standards while responding thoughtfully to the cultural and climatic character of Maharashtra’s Marathwada region."

The Vision

Hyatt Place Chhatrapati Sambhaji Nagar was envisioned as a contemporary hospitality destination that seamlessly blends business functionality with leisure comfort. The landscape design was thoughtfully developed to create welcoming outdoor environments, enhance guest movement and arrival experiences, and reflect the regional identity of Maharashtra’s Marathwada region through a modern landscape expression.

Key Highlights

Landscape architecture for Hyatt Place brand
Business hotel outdoor amenities and guest circulation
Regional landscape character in contemporary expression
Climate-responsive plant palettes and hardscape

Key Challenges

↗

Designing functional outdoor spaces that support both business travelers and leisure guests while maintaining Hyatt Place brand standards

↗

Creating a contemporary landscape identity that reflects the cultural and climatic context of the Marathwada region

↗

Integrating climate-responsive plantation and durable hardscape materials suitable for Chhatrapati Sambhaji Nagar’s environmental conditions

Project Brief

PROJECT TYPE Hospitality Hotel
LOCATION Chhatrapati Sambhaji Nagar, Maharashtra

Our Solutions

  • ✓ Integrated climate-responsive plantation and contemporary hardscape materials suitable for the regional environment
  • ✓ Created functional circulation spaces and outdoor gathering zones that enhance guest comfort and accessibility
