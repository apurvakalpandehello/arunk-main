HOSPITALITY RESORT | LANDSCAPE DESIGN

ITC Fortune Select, Lavasa

"A hillside retreat within India's planned hill city, where our landscape work integrates with Lavasa's master-planned environment. We created resort grounds that feel both part of the township's vision and distinctly tailored to ITC Fortune's hospitality experience."

The Vision

Hill station hospitality, reimagined. The landscape architecture for ITC Fortune Select in Lavasa was envisioned to create a cohesive balance between a high-end resort experience and the carefully curated urban fabric of the township. The goal was to extend the resort's functional outdoor spaces so they harmonize with the surrounding hill terrain, providing guests with a tranquil, immersive stay while honoring the architectural guidelines of this unique planned destination.

Key Highlights

Landscape design within master-planned township context
Hill station resort grounds optimization
Integration with Lavasa's urban design framework
Outdoor spaces enhancing ITC Fortune guest experience

Key Challenges

↗

Designing resort landscapes that satisfy both individual property luxury standards and broader township master-planning regulations.

↗

Ensuring site accessibility and fluid movement across complex, sloping hillside topography.

↗

Maintaining ecological balance while creating amenity-rich environments for high-frequency guest use.
