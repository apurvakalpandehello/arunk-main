INDUSTRIAL & CORPORATE | LANDSCAPE DESIGN

Unichem Laboratories Goa

"A pharmaceutical campus where landscape design supports innovation, functionality, and employee wellbeing through sustainable green environments."

The Vision

Unichem Laboratories, Goa was envisioned as a pharmaceutical research and manufacturing campus where landscape design enhances scientific innovation, employee wellbeing, and environmental integration. Our approach focused on creating sustainable outdoor environments that harmonize with Goa’s natural landscape while supporting the operational and corporate needs of the facility.

Key Highlights

Research facility landscape architecture
Integration with Goa's natural landscape character
Campus outdoor amenities and green infrastructure
Tropical landscape solutions for pharmaceutical sector

Key Challenges

↗

Designing landscapes that support pharmaceutical research and manufacturing functions while maintaining a professional campus identity

↗

Integrating outdoor amenities and green infrastructure within a highly functional research campus environment

↗

Developing tropical and climate-responsive landscape systems suitable for Goa’s environmental conditions
