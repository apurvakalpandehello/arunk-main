INDUSTRIAL & CORPORATE | LANDSCAPE DESIGN

ACG Worldwide Phase 1 Indore

"A pharmaceutical manufacturing campus where our landscape design creates a professional corporate environment. We balanced industrial functionality with employee wellbeing through efficient outdoor spaces."

The Vision

ACG Worldwide Phase 1, Indore was envisioned as a modern industrial and corporate campus where landscape design enhances operational efficiency, employee wellbeing, and corporate identity. Our approach focused on creating functional outdoor environments that balance industrial performance with green infrastructure and professional campus aesthetics.

Key Highlights

Industrial campus landscape architecture
Corporate branding through landscape design
Employee amenities and circulation planning
Low-maintenance, climate-appropriate planting strategies

Key Challenges

↗

Designing landscapes that support industrial operations while maintaining a professional campus identity

↗

Integrating employee amenities, circulation systems, and green spaces within a high-functioning manufacturing environment

↗
